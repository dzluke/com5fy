# M2.2: TimeMap (tempo and meter map), 2026-11-10

## What changed

**New: `m5.TimeMap`** ([mmmmm/src/m5/timemap.py](https://github.com/dzluke/mmmmm/blob/main/src/m5/timemap.py))
- Holds a piece's **tempo changes** and **meter changes**, plus a **pickup** (anacrusis) and the **number of the first
  measure** (for an excerpt that starts at bar 8, say).
- Converts between three kinds of time:
  - milliseconds: `ms_to_quarters` / `quarters_to_ms`
  - quarter notes: `quarters_to_measure_beat` / `measure_beat_to_quarters`
  - measure and beat: `ms_to_measure_beat` / `measure_beat_to_ms`
- Beats count from 1 (beat 1 is the downbeat, 2.5 is halfway through beat 2) in the meter's own unit, so 6/8 counts
  eighths. In a pickup, beats count back from the end of the bar: a one-quarter pickup in 4/4 is beat 4.
- A tempo keeps its beat unit: ♩. = 60 stays ♩. = 60 when it is written back, rather than becoming ♩ = 90.
- Where a map comes from:
  - a score: `Score.time_map` / `TimeMap.from_score`, from its metronome marks, time signatures and pickup measure
  - a MIDI file: `TimeMap.from_midi_file`, from its tempo and time-signature events (read with music21, so no new
    dependency)
  - the M2.4 MIDI type: `TimeMap.from_midi_events` / `to_midi_events`, which take ticks and work with any MIDI library
- `TempoChange`, `MeterChange` and `TimeMap` are exported from `m5`.

**A Sequence can carry its time map** ([time.py](https://github.com/dzluke/mmmmm/blob/main/src/m5/time.py),
[score.py](https://github.com/dzluke/mmmmm/blob/main/src/m5/score.py))
- `Sequence.time_map` is optional. Timings stay in milliseconds; the map only says how to read them as bars and beats.
- **Score to Sequence** stores the score's map on the Sequence.
- **Sequence to Score** writes the map back: metronome marks, time signatures in every part, and the pickup measure
  (bar 0). A score with tempo or meter changes therefore survives Score → Sequence → Score.
  - Before this change, the round trip estimated one tempo and wrote everything in 4/4.
  - In the M2.1 duo, the 120 BPM section now comes back exactly, instead of as approximate overlapping rhythms.
- The private `_tempo_map` in score.py is gone, and `DEFAULT_BPM` (120) now lives in `timemap.py`; `m5.DEFAULT_BPM`
  still works.

**Nodes** ([nodes/convert.py](../../nodes/convert.py))
- Sequence to Score, `bpm`:
  - **0** (the default) uses the Sequence's own tempo map when it has one. Otherwise it estimates one tempo, as before.
  - **Any other value** forces that single tempo. It keeps only the first time signature, because under a different
    tempo the later barlines would fall at other times.
- The descriptions and tooltips of Score to Sequence and Sequence to Score now say this.

## Known limitations

- **No gradual tempo changes.** Accelerando and ritardando are not modelled; every tempo change is instant. Tempo text
  without a number ("Allegro") uses music21's guess at a speed.
- **Measure numbers are computed, not read.** They come from the meters, the pickup and the first measure number.
  Irregular numbering in a score (repeats, a renumbered bar, a cadenza bar) is not followed. Repeats are not expanded.
- **Only the outer Sequence's map counts.** Nested Sequences share it. Other ways of making a Sequence (Orchidea, M2.6
  descriptors) don't set a map, so they still get an estimated tempo in 4/4.
- **A forced `bpm` drops later meters.** It keeps only the first time signature. Keeping the others would need them
  re-placed in ms, which is not done.
- **MIDI time signatures in the middle of a bar** cut that bar short, as sequencers read them, and start a new measure.
- **The pickup measure is not marked `implicit` in the MusicXML.** music21 writes it as measure 0 without the flag. The
  input file isn't marked either, and OSMD draws it fine.

## Feedback wanted

- **Forced `bpm`:** is keeping only the first time signature right? The alternative is to keep the Sequence's
  meters at their positions in time, which can put barlines in odd places.
- **Beat numbering:** beats count from 1 in the meter's own unit (6/8 has six eighth-note beats, not two dotted-quarter
  beats). Would you rather count compound meters in dotted beats?
- **Bars in the Sequence Viewer:** should it draw barlines and bar numbers from the time map?
- **Tempo ramps:** do you need accelerando and ritardando in the map before M2.4 (MIDI) and M2.7 (Origin)?
